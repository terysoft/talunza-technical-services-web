# Talunza Technical Services Web

Customer-facing web app for Talunza Technical Services in Chiredzi and the Lowveld.

## Positioning
**Install • Repair • Connect • Protect**

**One number. Many technical problems solved.**

Primary market: Chiredzi, with service coverage expanding through Triangle, Hippo Valley, Mkwasine, Boli, Chingele, Ngundu, Rutenga and surrounding Lowveld areas as field economics are validated.

## Initial service families
- Electrical tubing and installations
- Solar
- CCTV and alarms
- Starlink, Wi-Fi and networking
- Satellite installation
- Computer repair
- Refrigeration and domestic appliances
- Welding and fabrication
- Incubator fabrication and repair
- Mechanical and auto-electrical services

## Product direction
This is not only a brochure site. It will become the customer and operations interface for reporting problems, booking technicians, quoting, job tracking, evidence, warranty history, social proof and partner-technician orchestration.

The main Talunza corporate/software website remains separate and software-focused.

See `docs/product-brief.md` for the canonical V1 product brief.
