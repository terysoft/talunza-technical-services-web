# Talunza Technical Services Web

Customer-facing web app for Talunza Technical Services in Chiredzi and the Lowveld.

## Positioning
**Install • Repair • Connect • Protect • Make**

**One number. Many technical problems solved.**

Primary market: Chiredzi, with service coverage expanding through Triangle, Hippo Valley, Mkwasine, Boli, Chingele, Ngundu, Rutenga and surrounding Lowveld areas as field economics are validated.

## Customer-facing structure
Talunza Forge sits under the Technical Services customer experience as the manufacturing/product arm. The same website can therefore serve customers who need a technician and customers who want a Talunza-made product.

Technical Services handles installation, repair, troubleshooting, maintenance and field/custom fabrication.

Talunza Forge handles repeatable manufactured products such as phone/tablet stands, TV stands and mounts, articulated support systems, steel furniture, steel-and-timber furniture, shelving and equipment enclosures.

The Forge engineering/code repository remains separate where useful for product/BOM/manufacturing data, but it does not require a separate public website at this stage.

## Initial service families
- Electrical tubing, wiring and installations
- Solar, inverter and battery systems
- CCTV and alarms
- Starlink, Wi-Fi and networking
- Satellite installation
- Computer repair
- Software services
- Refrigeration and domestic appliances
- Welding, repair and custom fabrication
- Incubator fabrication and repair

## Product direction
This is not only a brochure site. It will become the customer and operations interface for reporting problems, booking technicians, product enquiries, quotations, job tracking, evidence, warranty history, social proof and partner-technician orchestration.

The main Talunza corporate/software website remains separate and should route physical technical-service and Forge customers here rather than duplicate this catalogue.
