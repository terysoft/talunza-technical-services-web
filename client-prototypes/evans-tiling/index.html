Our story

Experienced abroad. Rebuilding at home.

After working in South Africa, the team is now developing Evans Tiling & Renovations as a Zimbabwe-based tiling and renovation business. Chiredzi is the starting point: local homes, shops, lodges, offices and property upgrades.

We are not claiming a large Zimbabwe portfolio before we have earned one. Our goal is simple: bring the standards and experience we have gained into the local market, complete good work, photograph it properly, collect references and grow through reputation.

Services

Start with tiling. Grow into complete renovations.

The launch focus is high-quality tiling and closely related finishing work. Larger renovation packages will be taken on where the team can control workmanship and sequencing.

Floor & Wall Tiling

Homes, shops, offices, passages, kitchens, bathrooms, verandas and selected commercial spaces.

Bathroom Renovations

Strip-out, preparation, waterproofing, tiling and coordinated bathroom finishing.

Kitchen Tiling

Floors, splashbacks, wall finishes and refurbishment work.

Repairs & Re-tiling

Loose, damaged or cracked tile replacement, re-grouting and problem-area repairs.

Waterproofing & Preparation

Wet-area preparation, screed correction and substrate checks before tiling.

Renovation Finishing

Painting and selected finishing works around tiling and room-renovation projects.
