Our story

Experienced abroad. Rebuilding at home.

After working in South Africa, the team is now developing Evans Tiling & Renovations as a Zimbabwe-based specialist finishing business. Chiredzi is the starting point: homes, shops, lodges, offices and commercial property upgrades.

The focus is wider than floor tiles alone. We want to become known for premium tile installation, modern wall and feature cladding, bathrooms, kitchens, waterproofing and complete renovation finishing.

Services

Tiling, Cladding & Renovation Solutions.

From precision tile installation to decorative feature cladding and complete room upgrades, our services are built around clean workmanship and premium finishing.

Floor & Wall Tiling

Porcelain, ceramic, large-format and decorative tiling for homes, shops, offices, bathrooms, kitchens and commercial spaces.

Wall & Feature Cladding

Decorative interior and exterior cladding for feature walls, entrances, TV walls, columns, boundary walls, fireplaces and selected façades.

Stone-Look & Tile Cladding

Modern stone-look, split-face, porcelain and other cladding finishes designed to create premium architectural surfaces.

Bathroom Renovations

Strip-out, preparation, waterproofing, tiling, niches, feature cladding and coordinated bathroom finishing.

Kitchen Tiling & Splashbacks

Floors, splashbacks, wall tiling and feature finishes for modern kitchen upgrades.

Repairs & Re-tiling

Loose, damaged or cracked tile replacement, re-grouting, failed areas and renovation corrections.

Waterproofing & Preparation

Wet-area preparation, screed correction, substrate checks and waterproofing before final finishes.

Feature Walls & Decorative Finishes

TV walls, reception walls, shopfront features and statement surfaces combining tile, cladding and trims.

Renovation Finishing

Painting and selected finishing works around tiling, cladding and room-renovation projects.
