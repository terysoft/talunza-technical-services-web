Our story

Experienced Abroad. Rebuilding at Home.

Evans Tiling & Renovations is being built as a premium finishing business rooted in South African work experience and now focused on homes, lodges, shops, offices and commercial property upgrades in Chiredzi and the wider Lowveld.

The business is deliberately broader than basic tile laying. Our premium direction combines tiling, cladding, bathrooms, kitchens, waterproofing and renovation finishing with stronger site discipline and better visual detailing.
